# Performance result: find at 100,008 vectors on a 16-core desktop-class machine, 2026-11-14

## Promise

- Promise, in the product's words: "As a store grows, a vector search stays within the time the product promises" (architectural views, the paragraph "A growing store keeps its search time"). No figure promised.
- Counted test that guards it: `an_indexed_search_does_fewer_distance_evaluations_than_exact` (`v2/contextdb/tests/vectors_index.rs`) counts the vectors compared on a small store. None yet at 100,000 vectors.

## Measurement

- Date: 2026-11-14; revision measured: `0c708a979e9eb5cbb1dca142f514edf96656711e`, five runs, the median of the five printed
- Program and command: the passages measurement of `v2/numbers.sh`, run with the build wrapper that gives it the machine alone; lines `passages_100008_find_*`
- Data: 100,008 real search passages, each a vector of 768 numbers and five other columns, in eight partitions, built and then sealed; the ten nearest asked for, over one partition, over several, over all
- Passes and statistic: five passes in each run, five runs; p95 is the median over the five runs of the middle of a run's passes; recall is the share of the recorded true ten nearest found, over 100 questions; threads: 1; memory limit: 1,073,741,824 bytes (read session)
- Machine: AMD Ryzen 9 9955HX, 16 cores and 32 threads, 59 GiB of memory (a 16-core desktop-class build machine; not a user's machine); operating system: unknown; Rust toolchain: unknown; user's machine: no
- Settings that can change the result: search mode as named in the figures; build rustflags none; popcount counting
