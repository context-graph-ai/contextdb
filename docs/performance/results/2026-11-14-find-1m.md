## Measurement

- Date: 2026-11-14; revision measured: `0c708a979e9eb5cbb1dca142f514edf96656711e` for the sightings rows (five runs, the median of the five); the generated-vector rows were not taken at this revision and keep the figures of `7f150eed3afb284c2c02220431c3cb858635f361`

- Machine: AMD Ryzen 9 9955HX, 16 cores and 32 threads, 59 GiB of memory (a 16-core desktop-class build machine; not a user's machine); operating system: unknown; Rust toolchain: unknown; user's machine: no
- Settings that can change the result: search mode as named in the figures; build rustflags none
