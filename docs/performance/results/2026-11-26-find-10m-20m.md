## Limitations

- **Recall of the loose and unbounded shapes is 0.8037 at 10,000,000 sightings (643 of 800) and 0.8350 at 20,000,000 (668 of 800), in both modes.** The bar, tight and ties shapes have recall 1.0000. The product does not state a recall figure; this result does not claim the loose and unbounded shapes reach one.
- At 20,000,000 sightings the loose shape in automatic mode has a p95 of 224.918 ms, above 200 ms, and a max of 651.713 ms; its `never_right_within_200ms` is 1/20.
- The figures are of a probe tree with uncommitted changes, not of the code of the landing; a re-measurement at the landing revision was not made.
- Time figures are of this box and run only; no timed figure is a promise.
