## Measurement

- Date: 2026-11-26 (02:07 to 03:25 UTC); revision measured: `672c10078a71d8b24a81ede80490bf979b0bcc79` (the script's header records one uncommitted file, a script fix made after the run began; the run used the committed script)
- Program and command: `v2/numbers.sh` with `NUMBERS_LARGE=1`, `IMPORT=dump-large`, `CAMERA_SECONDS=60`; lines `sightings_5000000_*`
- Data: 5,000,000 generated sightings of 768 numbers in one partition, loaded, sealed and grouped, then reopened; 100 questions; recall is the share of the recorded true ten nearest found

- Machine: AMD Ryzen 9 9955HX, 16 cores and 32 threads, 59 GiB of memory (a desktop-class build machine, to itself during the run; not a user's machine); operating system: Ubuntu 26.04.1, Linux 7.0; Rust toolchain: unknown; user's machine: no

## Limitations

- Indexed search at 5,000,000 vectors has a p95 of 180.838 ms against a p50 of 3.571 ms; the exact search is 1,284.055 ms at p95. Both are slower than the figures of 2026-11-14 (indexed about 115 ms, exact about 865 ms); the cause was not looked for, and the previous figures are of another revision.
- The bound rows are the script's own lines; this result does not explain what "with", "without" and the "against" numbers count beyond their names.
- A single run on one machine; no timed figure is a promise.
